Designed with Intention

Where rustic heritage meets quiet luxury.

The Trailhead Cabin was envisioned as an antidote to modern overstimulation. We wanted to build a place where you can listen to the sound of cedar branches stirring in the wind, cook a slow meal on cast-iron griddles, and sleep deeply beneath massive Douglas fir beams.

Every piece of furniture inside has been hand-selected or custom built by local artisans right here in the Snoqualmie valley. The heavy wool throws are woven by a family-owned mill, the ceramics in our pantry are thrown by hand, and the heavy iron hardware was forged specifically for these hemlock doors.

"We built this space not to isolate you from nature, but to cocoon you within it."

Whether your day is spent tracking through wet fern trails or staying wrapped in linen watching rain roll across the Cascades, the property is designed to be your permanent solid harbor.

House Manual & Details

Preparedness & Comfort FAQ

Everything you need to know about navigating your deep-forest stay comfortably.

How do we check in and access the cabin?

The cabin is outfitted with a secure digital smart-lock. You will receive your personalized access code and exact driving directions 72 hours before your arrival. The entry is fully private, allowing you to settle in entirely on your own schedule.

Is the cabin accessible by standard vehicles during winter?

While our driveway is regularly cleared, we strongly advise using an all-wheel-drive or 4WD vehicle with winter-rated tires between November and March. Mountain weather can transition rapidly, bringing snow and heavy pine debris.

What materials are provided for the stone fireplace and cedar hot tub?

We keep our dry-wood shed fully stocked with seasoned cedar, hemlock, and fir logs. Natural fatwood starters, matches, and safety equipment are all kept in the mudroom. You will never need to chop or bring your own firewood.

Are pets allowed, and is the boundary secure?

We welcome up to two well-behaved dogs. While we have one acre of safely fenced wilderness surrounding the immediate patio, the surrounding old-growth woods are expansive and home to native wildlife, so off-leash exploration should be supervised.

What is your cancellation policy?

Full refunds are provided for cancellations made up to 14 days prior to check-in. For cancellations within 14 days, we provide a 50% refund or a full voucher credit to reschedule your stay within 12 calendar months.

Ready to seek the woods?

Secure your reservations for the season. Dates during autumn and winter fill months in advance.
