A Space to Unwind

Crafted for the art of doing nothing.

The Trailhead Cabin was envisioned as a deliberate antidote to the constant noise of modern routines. Hidden among massive, moss-draped hemlocks, our timber dwelling bridges the boundary between raw wilderness and quiet interior luxury.

Every beam was placed with intention. From the oversized cedar deck reaching out into the forest canopy to the massive dry-stack stone fireplace that centers the living hall, this is a home created to shelter your thoughts and foster deep, unhurried conversations.

The Specifications

Thoughtful proportions. Elemental comforts.

We believe that comfort shouldn't feel synthetic. The Cabin is designed with raw, honest materials that wear in rather than wear out, providing everything you need to retreat comfortably through any Pacific Northwest season.
