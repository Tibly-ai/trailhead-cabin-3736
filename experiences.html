The Wilderness Map

Exploring Index & Beyond

Our cabin sits as a gateway to the spectacular Snohomish County backcountry. Here are our absolute favorite secret spots, hiking trails, and riversides right outside our doorstep.

01

Heybrook Lookout Trail

A short, rewarding 2.6-mile forest climb leading to a historic wooden fire tower. Climb the tower steps for jaw-dropping, vertical views of Mount Index and Mount Persis.

02

The Skykomish River Pools

Quiet gravel riverbanks and turquoise glacier-fed pools perfect for crisp summer swimming, skipping stones, or enjoying an afternoon with a thermos of hot cider.

03

Barclay Lake Wilderness

A gentle forest walk under massive old-growth hemlocks and mossy cedar trees ending at a mirror-like lake beneath the dramatic, sheer cliffs of Baring Mountain.

"We wanted to design experiences that avoid tourist traps and instead invite you into the hidden, quiet rhythms of these mountains. Every spot on this list is a place we visit to clear our own minds."

— Your Hosts, Silas & Evelyn
